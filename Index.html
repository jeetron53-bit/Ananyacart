Privacy policy

Your trust matters to us. Here's a clear, simple look at how AnanyaCart collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information we collect

We collect the details you share with us when you browse, add items to your cart, or reach out on WhatsApp — such as your name, phone number, delivery address, and order preferences.

How we use it

  • To confirm and deliver your orders
  • To share delivery charges and order updates on WhatsApp
  • To improve our products and shopping experience
  • To respond to your questions and support requests

How we protect it

We never sell your personal information. Order and chat details shared on WhatsApp are used only to process your purchase and are kept confidential.
